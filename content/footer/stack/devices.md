## Overview

Home data only matters if it's accurate. The Spry device stack pairs FDA-cleared blood pressure monitoring, hospital-grade pulse oximetry, and clinical body composition tracking — the metrics your clinician actually reads.

No novelty wearables. No unvalidated sensors. These are the same categories used in outpatient clinics, selected for Bluetooth sync, app integration, and long-term reliability.

Use them standalone or alongside GLP-1, hypertension, and metabolic protocols. Readings sync to your health apps and are available for clinician review on request.
