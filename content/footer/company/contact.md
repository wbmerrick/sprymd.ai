## Overview

Questions about your protocol, account, privacy, or press? Use the channels below. SpryMD is a telehealth platform — we are not an emergency service. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room.

For general inquiries, use the form below or email us directly. We aim to respond within one business day.

## What we can't help with here

- **Medical emergencies** — Call 911 or seek immediate in-person care
- **Controlled substance requests outside protocol** — All medications require clinician authorization
- **Cases we've declined** — If telehealth wasn't appropriate, we'll provide guidance on next steps at the time of review
