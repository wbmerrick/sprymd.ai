## Overview

SpryMD is a clinician-led platform. Protocols are not generated by algorithms and dispensed without oversight — they are authorized, supervised, and adjusted by licensed physicians who exercise independent medical judgment on every case.

Our clinical team holds board certification, active state licensure, and years of experience across hospital, outpatient, and telehealth settings. Care is delivered under HIPAA-compliant infrastructure with the same standards you'd expect in a traditional practice — adapted for how men actually live.

We are not a pill mill. When telehealth is not the right setting, we decline. When it is, we move with rigor.

## Clinical standards

- **Board certification** — Active certification in a relevant specialty
- **State licensure** — Licensed in the state where care is delivered
- **Independent medical judgment** — No protocol is authorized without physician review
- **Evidence-based protocols** — Recommendations grounded in clinical guidelines and individual patient data
- **HIPAA compliance** — Protected health information handled under federal privacy and security requirements
- **Decline when inappropriate** — Cases outside telehealth scope, contraindicated therapies, or emergency presentations are not accepted

## Supervision model

SpryMD operates an asynchronous clinical review model:

1. Patient completes structured intake and labs
2. A licensed clinician reviews the full record — history, symptoms, lab values, and contraindications
3. If appropriate, a protocol is authorized; if not, the patient is informed with clear next steps
4. Ongoing monitoring triggers re-evaluation and protocol adjustments as needed

**Typical clinician review turnaround: within ~24 hours** of completed intake and lab results. Urgent or emergency concerns are directed to appropriate in-person or emergency care — SpryMD is not an emergency service.

## Pharmacy partnership

Compounded medications are fulfilled through **Empower Pharmacy**, a licensed compounding pharmacy. Prescriptions are written by SpryMD clinicians and dispensed under standard pharmacy regulations.
