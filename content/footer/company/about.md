## Overview

SpryMD is a clinician-supervised telehealth platform built for men who want more from medicine than a waiting room and a prescription pad. We connect assessment, interpretation, and optimization into one closed loop — so every protocol is grounded in data, reviewed by a licensed physician, and adjusted as your body responds.

This is not a pill mill. We practice performance medicine: evidence-based protocols for hormone optimization, metabolic health, recovery, and longevity — delivered with discretion, shipped through licensed partners, and monitored continuously. When a case isn't appropriate for telehealth, we say so. When it is, we move with precision.

Modern medicine, made for men. Operate at your ceiling.

## Mission

Help men access elite performance-health care without the friction of traditional systems — while holding the clinical bar where it belongs.

## The closed loop

Every SpryMD protocol follows the same architecture:

- **Assess** — Structured intake, labs, and health history reviewed before any recommendation
- **Interpret** — A licensed clinician evaluates results in context, not in isolation
- **Optimize** — Protocols are prescribed, compounded when appropriate, shipped discreetly, and refined through ongoing monitoring

No shortcuts. No anonymous ordering. No set-it-and-forget-it prescriptions.

## How we're different

- **Clinician-supervised** — Every protocol is reviewed and authorized by a board-certified physician
- **Continuous monitoring** — Labs, symptoms, and outcomes inform adjustments over time
- **Discreet fulfillment** — Compounded medications shipped through Empower Pharmacy and licensed partners
- **Clear boundaries** — We decline cases that aren't appropriate for telehealth; we are not emergency care

## Philosophy

Performance medicine should feel modern, direct, and honest. SpryMD exists to give men a system that matches their ambition — rigorous underneath, effortless on the surface.
