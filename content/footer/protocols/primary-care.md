## Overview

Performance medicine needs a primary care foundation. SpryMD Primary Care puts a dedicated licensed clinician on your account — the same provider who can oversee your protocols, order labs, manage chronic conditions, and coordinate referrals when specialist care is required.

Annual wellness, same-day secure messaging, and structured follow-up on one platform. No separate patient portal. No 3-week wait for a callback. Your clinician knows your chart because they are your clinician.

## Who it's for

- Men who want a dedicated PCP without the traditional office visit model
- Patients already on SpryMD protocols who need coordinated, longitudinal care
- Those who need annual wellness visits, lab review, or chronic condition management
- Households seeking shared clinician continuity for up to two adults

## What you get

- Dedicated SpryMD clinician assigned to your account
- Annual wellness visit and ongoing chronic care management
- Unlimited secure messaging with same-day response targets
- Lab ordering and interpretation (with PCP + Annual Labs tier)
- Referrals to specialists when in-person or subspecialty care is indicated
- Cancel anytime

## How it works

01. **Assess** — Complete intake and select your membership tier. Upload prior records if available.

02. **Clinician interpretation** — A licensed provider is assigned to your account. Initial visit establishes your baseline health picture and care plan.

03. **Continuous optimization** — Ongoing messaging, scheduled check-ins, annual wellness, and lab review. Your clinician coordinates across all active protocols.

## Protocol options

- **Spry Primary Care** — Full PCP membership with annual wellness, chronic care management, referrals, and unlimited secure messaging.
- **PCP + Annual Labs** — Primary Care membership plus comprehensive annual labs — lipids, A1C, CBC, and hormone panel as indicated.
- **PCP Essentials** — Lighter plan with messaging and as-needed visits. For patients who already have specialists.
- **Household Primary Care** — Shared membership for up to two adults. Same clinician continuity for you and a partner.

## Safety

Primary care through SpryMD covers routine and chronic needs — not emergency or acute hospital-level care. We decline membership when a patient's needs exceed what telehealth PCP can safely provide. SpryMD is not emergency care — call 911 for chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms, severe injury, or any acute medical emergency.
