## How it works

01. **Assess** — Complete a private intake covering symptoms, duration, prior treatments, and current medications.

02. **Clinician interpretation** — A licensed provider reviews your case, screens for contraindications and drug interactions, and selects the appropriate agent and starting dose.

03. **Continuous optimization** — Report response and side effects. Titrate dose or switch agents based on clinical guidance.

## Protocol options

- **Sertraline** — First-line SSRI for depression and anxiety. Broad evidence base, flexible titration 25–100 mg daily.
- **Escitalopram** — SSRI with a clean interaction profile. Often preferred for generalized anxiety.
- **Bupropion XL** — NDRI antidepressant. Strong fit when energy, focus, or SSRI sexual side effects are a concern.
- **Buspirone** — Non-sedating anxiolytic. No dependence risk. Pairs well with SSRIs.
- **Fluoxetine** — Long half-life SSRI. Useful for obsessive features or patients who miss occasional doses.

## Safety

We screen for bipolar disorder, active suicidal ideation with plan or intent, substance use disorders requiring higher-level care, and medication interactions. Treatment is declined when asynchronous telehealth is not clinically appropriate — including acute psychiatric crises. SpryMD is not emergency care. If you are in crisis, call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or 911 immediately.
