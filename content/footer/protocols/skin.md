## Overview

Prescription skincare works when the active ingredients are at therapeutic concentrations and compounded for tolerability. SpryMD skin protocols use tretinoin-based complexes, acne gels, and anti-aging formulations — all compounded by Empower Pharmacy with adjuvants that reduce irritation without diluting efficacy.

Whether you are starting retinoids for the first time or escalating to a multi-active acne protocol, a licensed clinician reviews your skin type, goals, and tolerance before writing the prescription. No counter consultation. No guesswork.

## Protocol options

- **Tretinoin TAAN Mild** — Tretinoin 0.03%, azelaic acid 4%, niacinamide 4%. Three-active complex for first-time retinoid users.
- **Tretinoin TAAN Strong** — Tretinoin 0.045% with azelaic acid and niacinamide. For retinoid-tolerant patients.
- **Acne Ultra Gel** — Clindamycin 1%, niacinamide 4%, tretinoin 0.02%. Three-mechanism acne treatment.
- **Acne Extreme Gel** — Clindamycin, dapsone, niacinamide, and tretinoin. Four-active formula for moderate-severe acne.
- **Anti-Aging Vita Gel** — Niacinamide 4%, tretinoin 0.03%, vitamin C 5%. Retinoid and antioxidant in one stable compound.

## Safety

We screen for pregnancy, eczema flares, active skin infections, and contraindications to topical antibiotics or retinoids. Treatment is declined when in-person dermatologic evaluation is more appropriate. SpryMD is not emergency care — call 911 for signs of severe allergic reaction, angioedema, or any acute medical emergency.
