Low testosterone is a lab-confirmed diagnosis, not a marketing funnel. SpryMD hormonal protocols require baseline labs, clinician interpretation, and scheduled follow-up — because exogenous testosterone and fertility-preserving alternatives both demand monitoring.

From injectable testosterone cypionate to enclomiphene for men who want to preserve fertility, every protocol is supervised by a licensed clinician and compounded by Empower Pharmacy. Dose titration and repeat labs are part of the system, not an upsell.

## How it works

01. **Assess** — Complete intake covering symptoms, medical history, prior hormone therapy, and fertility goals. Submit or order baseline labs.

02. **Clinician interpretation** — A licensed provider reviews your labs and clinical picture, then selects the protocol, starting dose, and monitoring schedule.

03. **Continuous optimization** — Repeat labs at defined intervals. Adjust dose, add HCG or aromatase control as clinically indicated.

## Protocol options

- **Testosterone Cypionate** — Gold-standard injectable TRT. Weekly protocol, titrated 100–200 mg with scheduled follow-up labs.
- **Enclomiphene** — Oral selective estrogen receptor modulator. Raises endogenous testosterone while preserving fertility.
- **Testosterone Cream** — Transdermal daily application. Steady levels without injections.
- **TRT + HCG Stack** — Testosterone cypionate with HCG support to maintain testicular function and fertility markers.
- **Low-T Optimization Pack** — Enclomiphene with PRN anastrozole. Entry protocol for borderline levels.

## Safety

Baseline and follow-up labs are required. We screen for prostate disease, polycythemia risk, untreated sleep apnea, and contraindications to hormone therapy. Treatment is declined when labs or clinical findings do not support therapy, or when monitoring cannot be maintained. SpryMD is not emergency care — call 911 for chest pain, shortness of breath, sudden severe headache, or any acute medical emergency.
