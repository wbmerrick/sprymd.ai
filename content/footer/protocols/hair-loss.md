## Overview

Androgenetic hair loss is progressive — and treatable when you intervene early with the right agents. SpryMD hair protocols combine DHT blockade, vasodilation, and compounded topicals formulated by Empower Pharmacy for patients who want oral simplicity or localized application.

From finasteride monotherapy to five-agent topical stacks, every regimen is clinician-reviewed and matched to your pattern, tolerance, and treatment history. Consistency beats novelty. We build protocols you can maintain.

## Who it's for

- Men with male-pattern hair loss (receding hairline, crown thinning, or diffuse shed)
- Patients who want oral finasteride, oral minoxidil combinations, or topical alternatives
- Those who have plateaued on single-agent OTC regimens
- Anyone seeking clinician-supervised treatment without a dermatology waitlist

## What you get

- Licensed clinician review before prescription
- Protocol matched to oral, topical, or stacked preference
- Prescriptions compounded by Empower Pharmacy and shipped discreetly
- Secure messaging for side effects and regimen adjustments
- Topical options that minimize systemic DHT blockade when preferred
- Cancel anytime

## How it works

01. **Assess** — Complete intake covering hair loss pattern, duration, prior treatments, and reproductive plans.

02. **Clinician interpretation** — A licensed provider reviews your case, screens for contraindications, and selects the protocol and formulation.

03. **Continuous optimization** — Track response over months. Escalate to stronger compounds or combination stacks when clinically appropriate.

## Protocol options

- **Finasteride 1mg Oral** — Daily DHT blockade. Gold-standard first-line treatment.
- **Finasteride + Oral Minoxidil** — Dual oral protocol combining DHT blockade with systemic vasodilation.
- **Hair Restore MFK Topical** — Minoxidil 5%, finasteride 0.3%, ketoconazole 2%. Three-agent topical with reduced systemic absorption.
- **Hair Restore Extreme Topical** — Five-agent compound: minoxidil, dutasteride, finasteride, tretinoin, ketoconazole. Maximum-strength for plateaued patients.
- **Biotin / Fin / Min Capsule** — Three actives in one daily oral capsule. Simplest multi-agent regimen.

## Safety

We screen for liver disease, pregnancy exposure risk (finasteride is teratogenic — partners must not handle crushed tablets if pregnant), and contraindications to minoxidil or topical agents. Treatment is declined when the risk profile is unfavorable. SpryMD is not emergency care — call 911 for signs of allergic reaction, chest pain, or any acute medical emergency.
