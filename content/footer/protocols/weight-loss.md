## Overview

Metabolic weight loss is not a willpower problem — it is a signaling problem. SpryMD GLP-1 protocols use tirzepatide and semaglutide, compounded by Empower Pharmacy and titrated by a licensed clinician to your response, not a one-size dose chart.

Choose injectable protocols with overnight cold-chain delivery, oral dissolve tablets for needle-free microdosing, or the Bella Metabolic Stack when injectables are not the right fit. Every prescription is reviewed before it ships. Dose adjustments and follow-up are built into the protocol.

## How it works

01. **Assess** — Complete a structured intake covering medical history, current weight, prior GLP-1 use, and goals. Upload labs if you have them.

02. **Clinician interpretation** — A licensed provider reviews your case, confirms eligibility, and selects the protocol and starting dose. We decline when clinical risk outweighs benefit.

03. **Continuous optimization** — Track progress, report side effects, and request titration. Your clinician adjusts the protocol as you respond.

## Protocol options

- **Tirzepatide Injection** — Dual GIP/GLP-1 agonism. Weekly subcutaneous protocol, titrated 5–15 mg. Strongest agent in the catalog.
- **Semaglutide Injection** — GLP-1 receptor agonist. Weekly protocol, titrated 0.5–1 mg. Proven, affordable entry point.
- **Tirzepatide ODT** — Daily oral dissolve microdose. No needles, no refrigeration.
- **Semaglutide ODT** — Same active as the injection in a once-daily melt-in-mouth format.
- **Tirzepatide High-Dose** — Maintenance-tier protocol for patients who have titrated through standard ranges.
- **Bella Metabolic Stack** — Five-agent oral compound (bupropion, phentermine, topiramate, naltrexone, B12) for patients who cannot or prefer not to use injectables.

## Safety

Every patient is screened for contraindications including personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma, MEN2, pancreatitis, and pregnancy. We decline treatment when GLP-1 therapy is not clinically appropriate. SpryMD is not emergency care — seek immediate help or call 911 for severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, signs of allergic reaction, or any acute medical emergency.
